# Rights and attribution

This repository combines third-party source data and author-constructed transformations. It is not a public-domain collection. Source-specific conditions continue to apply; no blanket CC0 or MIT licence is asserted over the data.

## Binance-derived data

Source: Binance Vision, https://data.binance.vision/ . The dataset terms reviewed on 17 November 2026 identify CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for datasets unless otherwise designated and require attribution and the same licence for redistributed derivatives. The repository is prepared for noncommercial academic use. Consult the provider terms for their full conditions.

- Dataset terms: https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
- Transformations: date selection, UTC calendar matching, weekly sums and volatility, natural logarithms, lag/lead variables, taker-volume pressure and Bitcoin return-based states.

Binance-derived CSVs and the Binance-derived components of combined files retain those terms. Other providers' rights are not replaced by the Binance licence.

## Google Trends

Data source: Google Trends, https://trends.google.com/ . Credit this source when reusing the indices or exports. Local transformations include linking overlapping windows, normalization, weekly differences, abnormal attention and calendar matching. Retain source attribution and comply with Google's applicable terms.

- Guidance: https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365538?hl=en
- Terms: https://policies.google.com/terms

## Data omitted pending redistribution clearance

No Datastream or Bitstamp observations are included. VIX and UCRY values are supplied separately to the researcher, outside this public folder. FRED explicitly identifies VIX observations as copyrighted. The UCRY provider permits use with citation, but an explicit redistribution licence for the updated supplied file was not established. The public folder provides source and acquisition information instead.

No GitHub publication has been performed by preparing this folder. Do not upload the separate researcher-only uncertainty supplement as part of this public repository without appropriate clearance. This note records the source conditions reviewed; it is not a grant of rights on behalf of any provider.
